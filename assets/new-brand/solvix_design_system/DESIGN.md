## Brand & Style

This design system embodies methodical problem-solving, structural clarity, and high-impact digital execution. Built upon a direct conceptual linear progression—**PROBLÈME → RÉFLEXION → SOLUTION**—the visual language eliminates unnecessary embellishment to highlight substance, architectural discipline, and pragmatic innovation.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Pragmatic & Decisive:** Clear visual hierarchies, deliberate editorial rhythms, and immediate scannability project technical mastery and absolute competence.
- **Architectural & Intentional:** Layouts are grounded by strong horizontal and vertical structures that evoke balance, stability, and rigor.
- **Human-Centric & Modern:** Confident typography paired with generous white space creates a focused, calm, yet high-energy atmosphere.
- **Anti-Gimmick Posture:** Rejects synthetic trends—strictly zero generic neon glows, violet/cyan gradients, or gratuitous 3D artifacts. Technology is framed as an instrument of purposeful execution.

### Emblematic Brand Cue: The Intersecting "X"
Derived from the geometric intersection mark, the brand iconography utilizes precise 45-degree angles, intersecting pathways, and framed structural cutouts. The signature "X" represents resolution and crossing the bridge between ambiguity and clarity. It is applied selectively as architectural framing devices, process connectors, and structural watermark grids.

## Colors

The color architecture strictly enforces the 60-30-10 distribution model, ensuring purposeful optical hierarchy, effortless readability, and decisive focal attention.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (60%) — `#FFFFFF` (Pure White):** The primary operating canvas. Generates breathing room, absolute contrast, and crystalline focus around core strategic narratives.
- **Structural Body (30%) — `#333333` (Architectural Anthracite):** Governs structural framing, headings, primary body copy, dividing rules, and high-contrast navigational footers. Softened slightly from pure pitch-black to reduce optical fatigue while preserving authoritative weight.
- **Active Accent (10%) — `#FF6B4A` (Signature Coral):** Reserved strictly for strategic moments of conversion, interaction feedback, the conclusive "SOLUTION" phase, and primary action items. Never diluted into ornamental backgrounds.
- **Foundation Layer — `#F0F0F0` (Soft Pale Gray):** Deployed for structural sub-panels, secondary containers, input backdrops, and balanced contrast layers against pure white.

### Application Rules
1. **Strategic Accent Discipline:** Coral (`#FF6B4A`) must not exceed 10% of any viewport. It acts as an optical catalyst—directing the user's eye to conclusive actions and metrics.
2. **Text Contrast:** All high-order typographic elements must use `#333333` on `#FFFFFF` or `#F0F0F0`, achieving WCAG AAA contrast compliance.
3. **No Gradients:** Colors are applied in flat, confident planes. Visual depth is established through structure and placement, never through arbitrary color blending.

## Typography

The typographic system relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, balanced terminals, and modern proportions to establish clarity across complex analytical interfaces.

### Hierarchy & Function
- **Display & Headline Levels (Weight: 700 / 600):** Employs tight line height and negative letter tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to create monolithic editorial impact for statements, project definitions, and key metrics.
- **Body Text (Weight: 400):** Balanced at standard tracking with proportional line heights (1.5x to 1.55x) to guarantee fatigue-free readability across case studies and problem-solving breakdowns.
- **Labels & Categorical Trackers (Weight: 500 / 600):** Formats stage identifiers (e.g., `01 / PROBLÈME`, `02 / RÉFLEXION`, `03 / SOLUTION`) using upper-case styling with expanded letter tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) to ensure clear visual categorization.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt structural cadence, using expansive margins and calculated whitespace to convey precision and intellectual breathing room.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum 48px (`3rem`) outer margin. Maximum content container width capped at `1280px` for optimal typographic line lengths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px outer canvas margins.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins.

### Component Spacing Discipline
- **Micro Rhythms:** `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern tight sub-label alignments, badge paddings, and icon-to-text gaps.
- **Card & Form Density:** Standard container padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports.
- **Section Breaks:** Minimum vertical spacing between major structural narrative sections is fixed at 80px to 120px on desktop, scaling down to 48px on mobile screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated, heavily blurred artificial shadows in favor of a crisp architectural approach built on tonal layering and low-contrast borders.

### Surface Stratification
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` serves as the primary canvas for all major textual reading flows.
- **Secondary Tier (Structural Inset):** `#F0F0F0` containers provide grounded contrast for metadata sidebars, tabular data clusters, and comparative matrices.
- **High-Contrast Focal Tier:** `#333333` backdrops are used purposefully for concluding call-to-action blocks, executive executive summaries, or key navigational headers.

### Edge Definition & Subtle Depth
- **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines:** Surfaces are primarily differentiated via `1px solid #F0F0F0` or `1px solid rgba(51, 51, 51, 0.08)`.
- **Directional Grounding:** Where elevation is necessary to indicate interactive lifting (e.g., interactive cards on hover), use a razor-sharp ambient offset:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(51, 51, 51, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(51, 51, 51, 0.04)`.
- **Absolute Sharpness:** Blur radiuses never exceed 16px. No colored neon blurs or glow halos are permitted under any condition.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes architectural precision, purposeful angles, and refined restraint.

### Geometric Rules
- **Corner Curvature (`roundedness: 1`):** Base components (buttons, input fields, badges) maintain a tight `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius. Larger surfaces like cards and modals utilize `8px` (`0.5rem`). Circular or pill shapes are avoided to keep the interface grounded and architectural.
- **Intersection Language:** Structural elements borrow direct cues from the Solvix "X" mark: sharp 45-degree corner chamfers on bespoke containers, directional linear connectors, and strict rectangular framing.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Coral background (`#FF6B4A`), pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, 4px border-radius, horizontal padding `space-lg`, vertical padding `space-sm`. Hover state slightly darkens background by 6% with no vertical translation.
- **Secondary CTA:** Solid Anthracite background (`#333333`), pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), 4px border-radius.
- **Tertiary / Structural Button:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid border (`#333333`), text `#333333`. Hover applies `#F0F0F0` background fill.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #F0F0F0`, corner radius 4px, text `#333333`. On active focus: border transitions directly to `1px solid #FF6B4A` with an immediate `0 0 0 1px #FF6B4A` ring. Placeholder text uses `rgba(51, 51, 51, 0.4)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Anthracite `#333333` boundary in neutral state; fills with `#FF6B4A` on checked states with crisp white glyphs. Checkbox corners use 2px radius; radios are circular.

### Cards & Narrative Modules
- **Structured Cards:** Base `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #F0F0F0` border and 8px corner radius. Padding strictly set to `24px` (`space-lg`).
- **Tri-Fold Process Module (PROBLÈME → RÉFLEXION → SOLUTION):** 
  - *Problème Card:* Neutral white background, subtle left border indicator in `#333333`.
  - *Réflexion Card:* `#F0F0F0` background, highlighting analytical intermediate metrics.
  - *Solution Card:* High-contrast structural framing, incorporating Coral (`#FF6B4A`) accents on resolution highlights and conclusive metrics.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Phase Chips:** 24px height, uppercase `label-sm`, padding 0 8px, radius 2px. 
  - Standard chip: `#F0F0F0` background, `#333333` label.
  - Solution chip: Light coral wash background (`rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.1)`), `#FF6B4A` text.
